### Desktop app

- The window is now three numbered steps: 1. Add your designs, 2. Choose where to save them, 3. Pick how to convert them.
- Adding designs is grouped by source (From this computer, From a link, From Onshape), the link field and Add link share a row, and Clear sits under the list of added designs.
- Save to shows the output folder with its start and end when it is long, and the conversion options put each label before its control: Preset, Advanced, Output format, Existing files, Checks.
- The header's buttons (Settings, the SolidCraft version, Update, Donate, Discord) share one shape and height, with the theme choices and Report a problem on the same line.
- The header has Donate and Discord buttons beside Settings; each opens its page in your browser and can be hidden with its own checkbox in Settings.
