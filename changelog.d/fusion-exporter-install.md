### Desktop app

- Export from Fusion: Dutchman finds the Fusion exporter add-in running in your Fusion, shows "Fusion is busy" while Fusion is occupied with a large design, and keeps waiting for it.
- When the exporter is not found on Windows or macOS, one button, "Install the Fusion exporter", downloads the pinned release, checks its SHA-256, puts it in Fusion's Add-Ins folder, and shows the steps to switch it on once in Fusion.
