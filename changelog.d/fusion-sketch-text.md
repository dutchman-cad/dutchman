### History

- Fusion sketch text now maps to SolidCraft sketch text instead of blocking its sketch: the words, font, bold, baseline start, angle, height and left, centre or right and top, middle or bottom alignment are kept, and SolidCraft sets the letters. Width scaling, a medium weight and top or bottom anchoring that can sit differently in SolidCraft's font box are reported as degraded notes. A text with no stored placement (one laid out along a curve) is left out of its sketch with a degraded note, and the rest of the sketch maps.
