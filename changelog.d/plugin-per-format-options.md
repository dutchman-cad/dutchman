### Desktop app

- The SolidCraft plugin remembers Keep editable history, Up axis, and Include hidden bodies
  separately for Fusion, Inventor, SolidWorks, and FreeCAD files, and offers the choices for
  the format of the file you pick. A format you have not imported yet gets your latest choices.
- The **Folder with the parts** field appears only for files that can have parts: `.iam`,
  `.sldasm`, `.f3d`, and `.f3z`.
