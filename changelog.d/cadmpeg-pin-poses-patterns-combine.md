### cadmpeg

- The cadmpeg pin moves from `a5ccc9d` to `e06fa52`. Fusion places local occurrences at the
  root of a design, and external occurrences, at the pose the design shows them in, from the
  records that store that pose, instead of where they were inserted; when those records
  disagree, the ones that list the most owners decide. Inventor rectangular feature patterns
  with fixed spacing, in one or two directions, convert as SolidCraft rectangular patterns;
  Combine is read as a Boolean of bodies, which Dutchman reports as not mapped yet. SolidWorks
  parts that fall back to their display meshes keep them, and report that the meshes carry no
  exact geometry.
