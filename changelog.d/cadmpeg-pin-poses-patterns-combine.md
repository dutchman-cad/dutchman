### cadmpeg

- The cadmpeg pin moves from `a5ccc9d` to `e06fa52`. Fusion places local occurrences at the root
  of a design, and external occurrences, at the pose the design shows them in, from the records
  that store that pose, instead of where they were inserted; when those records disagree, the
  ones that list the most owners decide. Inventor reads rectangular feature patterns with fixed
  spacing in one or two directions as linear patterns, and Combine as a Boolean of bodies (cut,
  join, or intersect, keeping or consuming the tools). SolidWorks parts that fall back to their
  display meshes keep them, and report that the meshes carry no exact geometry.
