### Assemblies

- Keep mixed face colours on cones, spherical corners and resolved curved trim surfaces, including NURBS and periodic torus bands.
- Use resolved trim charts before vertex-only picks, keeping long cylindrical arcs and planar holes on the right faces.
- Keep solid colours when a component also contains saved wire edges or an unwritable sibling body.
- Keep appearance bindings under their complete component and instance identities in the shared component index.
- Apply final-shape colours to the visible Import beside a hidden editable history copy.
- Identify missing appearance targets explicitly and distinguish them from unplaced or deliberately filtered geometry.
