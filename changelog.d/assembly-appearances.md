### Assemblies

- Fewer false "colour dropped" notes on Fusion assemblies: colour assignments that do reach the design are no longer reported as lost, including on parts placed more than once and beside a hidden editable history copy.
- Fix wrong face colours on painted parts such as stepper motors, where a face colour landed on the wrong arc of a cylinder or inside a hole.
- Keep body colours when a component also contains saved wire edges or a body that cannot be written.
- Say plainly when a colour's target is missing from the decoded design.
