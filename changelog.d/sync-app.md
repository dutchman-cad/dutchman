### Desktop app

- The output folder now lists the designs whose source file changed since they were converted, under "Changed since last convert", with the program each comes from and "Source changed" or "Source missing". It looks when the output folder changes, after a run, and when you press Refresh. "Re-convert changed" converts each changed source again with the options its link record holds and shows a card for it; a design that fails keeps its previous `.sc`.
