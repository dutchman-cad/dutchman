### Desktop app

- A drawing file whose sheets are not read yet is labeled "Not supported yet" in the warning color instead of "Failed", and the summary counts it as "not supported yet" rather than failed. Its card says one thing, that the drawing is recognized but reading its sheets isn't supported yet, in the design list and on the result.
- The card of such a drawing no longer offers "Add matching STEP"; its help step asks for a PDF or DXF export of the same drawing.
