### Desktop app

- Add "Export from Fusion…" on Windows and macOS: list your Fusion designs through the Fusion exporter, pick them in a searchable hub, project, folder and design list, follow the export with a Stop button, and convert the finished folder. Failed designs are counted and can be retried, and every reason an export can fail is explained in plain words.
  It waits for Fusion while a list or an export runs and gives up with a plain message after a minute without Fusion, with Stop waiting to leave sooner.
