### cadmpeg

- Convert SolidWorks assemblies and group their referenced parts in projects without a duplicate document root.
- Keep independent Fusion archive members checkable during minimum-reproduction inspection.
- Refuse an assembly that places components inside a component it does not place, instead of leaving them out, and report an unresolved linked component as left out instead of failing the whole conversion.
- Keep the history of a Fusion root component that cadmpeg states without an owner, and no longer list a SolidWorks assembly as its own reference.
- Keep a suppressed insertion of a shared sub-assembly apart from one that shows the same component.
