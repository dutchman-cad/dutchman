Dutchman Convert 0.2.9 supports SolidCraft 0.41 as its newest target, with designs that open in SolidCraft 0.18.2 to 0.41. Verification now checks each rebuilt body's position and bounding box as well as volume and area, so a history that rebuilds a body in the wrong place is no longer reported as verified. More SolidWorks geometry converts, more Fusion history can stay editable, and sketches used across components reach more placed parts. Files are read with cadmpeg 0.6.11.

## What's new

- **SolidCraft 0.41 support.** SolidCraft 0.41 is the newest target, and designs open in 0.40 as well. Designs still open in SolidCraft 0.18.2, with features simplified for older targets where needed.
- **Tighter verification.** Rebuilt histories with large differences in body position or bounding box are no longer reported as verified, even when volume and area match. Kept partial histories are checked more tightly against where the source has each body; a feature that rebuilds a body in the wrong place is no longer kept. Incomplete evidence for a source body's centre leaves a partial history unverified.
- **More complete SolidWorks parts.** Closed faces without edges and ring edges are kept, and more offset surfaces convert. Source bodies used by derived or mirrored geometry no longer appear as extra current bodies. Parts convert in their saved configuration, leaving out bodies that belong only to other configurations and reporting which configuration was converted.
- **More history to rebuild.** More Fusion threads, holes, extrudes, fillets, shells, lofts, pipes, patterns, and healed face deletions are read for history mapping. Sketches keep their timeline names. More Inventor fillets, revolves, body trims, and rectangular body patterns are read for mapping too; reading a feature does not guarantee it can be rebuilt.
- **Sketches reach more parts.** A feature can use a sketch from another component when both components are placed once, or when exactly one instance puts the selected profile on a repeatedly placed part. Ambiguous placements leave the sketch out. Trimmed regions and regions with crossing holes are found more reliably, unused curves become construction geometry, and splines SolidCraft cannot take directly are kept as exact pieces where supported.
- **Refuse shapes that could be wrong.** A loft section taking several loops of one sketch is no longer rebuilt when that could add a hole the source does not have. An unresolved Fusion profile no longer selects every region of a divided sketch, which could cut away the whole part. Lofts needing split splines as sections or guide rails are left unmapped because the extra edges could change the surface.
- **Clearer progress, and no endless runs.** A design that makes no progress for 20 minutes, or runs 60 minutes in all, is stopped, in the desktop app too; in a Fusion `.f3z` archive it's left out and the rest converts, and the report names it (set `DUTCHMAN_DESIGN_TIME_LIMIT` to more minutes to give a large design longer). Progress names the running part or stage, shows its elapsed time, and explains what happens when a check reaches its limit. In folder runs, the desktop shows the longest-running file and how many others are running. Investigation can compare a conversion with nightly and candidate builds and produce a shareable summary without design names, paths, or sizes.

## Known issue

In SolidCraft 0.40, a loft whose first and last sections are regions of the same sketch has no mass. It opens correctly in SolidCraft 0.41 and 0.30 ([tangency-labs/solidcraft#15263](https://github.com/tangency-labs/solidcraft/issues/15263)).
