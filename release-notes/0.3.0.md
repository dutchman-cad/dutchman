Dutchman Convert 0.3.0 opens Fusion, Inventor, SolidWorks and FreeCAD files straight from SolidCraft with the new Dutchman plugin, and reads Onshape for the first time. SolidCraft 0.44 is the newest target, with designs that open in SolidCraft 0.18.2 to 0.44. Much more Fusion history stays editable, and the desktop app makes it easy to tell us when something is wrong.

## What's new

- **Dutchman inside SolidCraft.** Install the Dutchman plugin from Preferences > Plugins in SolidCraft 0.44 or newer (desktop), then open Fusion, Inventor, SolidWorks and FreeCAD files directly, with editable history. SolidCraft's Import report lists what could not be carried over in plain lines, and the plugin checks the history in SolidCraft itself, so a history that matches the original opens as the visible model. Each release attaches `dutchman-plugin-X.Y.Z.zip`.
- **Onshape support.** Connect your Onshape account from the app or with `dutchman onshape login`, using your own API keys or an OAuth bearer token, then paste a Part Studio link or use `dutchman onshape fetch`. Dutchman saves an offline bundle beside the converted design and keeps sketches, extrudes, revolves, fillets and chamfers (including tangent-propagated ones), tapped and blind holes, thin walls, symmetric extrudes and variables editable. Derived Part Studios stay linked to local SolidCraft designs. Anything not supported yet keeps the final shape and says why.
- **SolidCraft 0.44 support.** SolidCraft 0.44 is the newest target. On 0.44 and newer, extrudes from or to a flat face at a slant to the sketch, 3D sketch dimensions and relations, and more multi-body joins, cuts and intersections stay editable. On 0.44.1 and newer, fillets and chamfers on a full circle, such as the rim of a hole, stay editable too.
- **More Fusion history stays editable.** Chamfers with two different distances, contour flanges, Split Body, Copy/Paste Bodies, mirrored-then-removed bodies, symmetric patterns, Base Features that later steps change, clockwise sketch arcs, narrow profile regions such as belt teeth, surface revolves of open curves, and sweep paths made of connected edges all keep their history. An extrude whose profile Fusion does not record is kept when exactly one sketch region, rebuilt in SolidCraft, matches the original, and the report marks it `inferred`. Tapped blind holes keep their thread length and hand (SolidCraft 0.40 and newer) and an ISO tolerance class (0.43 and newer), and holes on box primitives and on faces with rounded corners convert.
- **Tell us when something is wrong.** "Is there a problem? Is something missing?" is always in the desktop window: join our Discord, DM Dutchy the file, or open a GitHub issue. Report this file… on each result opens it for that file. Partial and geometry-only results show in the warning colour, and Stop ends a conversion promptly.
- **Paste, share links and a look first.** Paste files, paths, Onshape links or HTTPS CAD file links into the app, or paste a public Fusion or Google Drive link to download and convert it. `dutchman convert <url>` works too. Links to private, local or cloud-metadata addresses are refused. `dutchman contents` and the MCP `contents` tool list a file's bodies, timeline and parameters, and which features map to SolidCraft, without converting; conversions show which feature they are on.
- **More formats, options and fixes.** Siemens NX parts convert their geometry. `--stand-up` turns Y-up models, such as older Fusion designs, into SolidCraft's Z-up. A SolidWorks assembly whose parts cannot be found says so. Reports carry the exact verification tolerances used, link each history row to the features it produced, and can be counts only. On Windows the command line no longer crashes with a stack overflow (exit 0xC0000409), choosing a `solidcraft-cli` older than 0.18.2 says so, and designs with surface bodies are checked against the original again on SolidCraft 0.23 and newer.

## Known issues

- A kept feature can replay from the wrong sketch region and still be counted as kept; the final shape is unaffected, because it comes from the source design ([dutchman-cad/dutchman#489](https://github.com/dutchman-cad/dutchman/issues/489)).
- Some large Fusion `.f3d` designs reach the 900 s limit for one file while being read ([dutchman-cad/dutchman#604](https://github.com/dutchman-cad/dutchman/issues/604)).

Versions: cadmpeg at commit f1338aa2, SolidCraft 0.18.2 to 0.44 (0.44 newest).
