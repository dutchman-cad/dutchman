Dutchman Convert 0.2.10 supports SolidCraft 0.43 as its newest target, with designs that open in SolidCraft 0.18.2 to 0.43. More Fusion history stays editable, Fusion holes on a cylinder and on earlier faces convert, FreeCAD output handles parts with several bodies, and Linux gets an AppImage and an Arch package. Files are read with cadmpeg 0.6.12.

## What's new

- **SolidCraft 0.43 support.** SolidCraft 0.43 is the newest target. Designs still open in SolidCraft 0.18.2, with features simplified for older targets where needed. Fusion bodies you hid stay hidden in SolidCraft 0.42 and newer.
- **More Fusion history stays editable.** Combine, Sweep, path pattern, sphere, Emboss, contour flange, unfold, refold, body mirror and split steps are read, and history continues past As-built joints, copied or moved components, and steps that change no geometry. Constant-radius fillets and equal-setback chamfers on several bodies keep one blend per body.
- **More Fusion holes and faces.** A Hole placed on a face of the model as it was before the hole converts, and so does a radial Hole drilled into a whole cylinder. An extrude of an earlier body's face is found when the face has small rounded corners, and a fillet or chamfer after an extrude that grows away from the body finds its edges.
- **Inventor sheet metal.** Flanges with their default height setting keep their editable history as SolidCraft edge flanges, and a corner that leaves its part unchanged no longer stops it.
- **FreeCAD output.** Parts that end as several bodies are rebuilt with a Body for each new-body feature, and a dimension driven by a parameter rebuilds with the parameter's value instead of stopping with "syntax error".
- **Clearer results.** A fillet or chamfer whose edge cannot be found now says why. A Fusion design that holds only mesh bodies says how many it has and that meshes are not converted yet. Dutchman Web uploads work in browsers again, and its results are easier to read. `--json` also works before the command.
- **Linux downloads and Docker.** Each release has a Linux AppImage for x86_64 and ARM64, an Arch Linux package, and `ghcr.io/dutchman-cad/dutchman:0.2.10-solidcraft`, an image that includes SolidCraft's checker so histories are checked with no build step. The AppImage updates itself from the desktop app.

## Known issues

- A kept feature can replay from the wrong sketch region and still be counted as kept; the final shape is unaffected, because it comes from the source design ([dutchman-cad/dutchman#489](https://github.com/dutchman-cad/dutchman/issues/489)).
- Some large Fusion `.f3d` designs reach the 900 s limit for one file while being read ([dutchman-cad/dutchman#604](https://github.com/dutchman-cad/dutchman/issues/604)).

Versions: cadmpeg 0.6.12 (pin 9249a19), SolidCraft 0.18.2 to 0.43 (0.43 newest).
