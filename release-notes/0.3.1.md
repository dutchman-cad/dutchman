Dutchman Convert 0.3.1 keeps more Fusion history editable, tells you plainly when parts of a design couldn't be placed, and targets SolidCraft 0.46.

## What's new

- **More history stays editable.** Offset Face no longer stops when the body it moves is known under two names, and Move Body now maps to SolidCraft's body move. On the RailCore 300ZL, 300ZLT and ZL v1 printers, 9, 9 and 21 more features stay editable; the Keystone Cutter now rebuilds 6 of its 8 features instead of 2.
- **Honest results for parts that couldn't be placed.** When Dutchman can't read where a part sits, the result now says "N parts couldn't be placed (their position in the archive couldn't be read)", in single Fusion designs made of components too, instead of calling the conversion verified.
- **Changed for scripts: unplaced parts make a conversion fail.** A conversion with parts that couldn't be placed still writes the design, but `dutchman convert` now exits with status 1 and its JSON report has `"ok": false`, with the count in `completeness.unplaced`. Scripts that treated any written design as success should check for this.
- **SolidCraft 0.46.** SolidCraft 0.46 is the newest target. Designs still open in SolidCraft 0.18.2 and newer, and the plugin keeps working in 0.45 and 0.46.
- **Fewer wrong "verified" results.** A replayed history whose shape or topology differs from the original, including bodies made of several separate pieces, is now refused instead of passing, and the visible model falls back to the original shape.
- **Faster checks.** An unchanged written design is evaluated in SolidCraft only once.

## Known issues

- Some Fusion assemblies, for example ones whose parts are linked inside the archive, report "N parts couldn't be placed": reading those positions is next ([dutchman-cad/dutchman#858](https://github.com/dutchman-cad/dutchman/issues/858), [cadmpeg/cadmpeg#1880](https://github.com/cadmpeg/cadmpeg/issues/1880)).
- SolidCraft's default plugin fuel limit (Preferences > Plugins > Max plugin fuel, 100,000,000,000) is too low for large assemblies: the plugin then stops with a fuel error. Raise it (700,000,000,000 opens a 41-part arm).
- SolidCraft 0.46.0 is a prerelease; SolidCraft 0.45 stays fully supported.
