Dutchman Convert 0.3.1 tells you what an Onshape fetch will cost, shows a partly converted history in the timeline, and keeps more Fusion and Inventor history editable.

## What's new

- **Onshape fetches say what they cost.** Before it spends anything, the fetch estimates its API calls (Onshape's Free plan allows 2,500 a year) and asks first above 50. Stop keeps what was fetched, Continue fetching sends only the missing calls, and the Results card counts calls as they go. Rows and files are named after your design, such as "Bracket - Part Studio 1".
- **A partly converted history is now visible.** The features Dutchman can rebuild come first in the timeline, then a Remove of their bodies, then "Rest of the design (from ...)" holding the original shape. The report says where it starts. Thanks to f15sim for the design that showed it.
- **More history stays editable.** Offset Face works when its body has two names, Move Body maps to SolidCraft's body move (0.45 and newer), holes that end at a flat face with curved edges, two-sided extrudes on one side of the sketch, and extrudes whose sketch lives in another design all rebuild. The RailCore 300ZL, 300ZLT and ZL v1 keep 9, 9 and 21 more features; the Keystone Cutter rebuilds 6 of 8 instead of 2.
- **Fusion tool libraries to CamCraft.** A `.tools` or `.json` library converts to a CamCraft `.camtools`; each tool or field CamCraft has no place for is named in the report.
- **Honest results for parts that couldn't be placed.** The result says "N parts couldn't be placed", also for a single design made of components, instead of calling it verified.
- **Changed for scripts:** such a conversion still writes the design, but `dutchman convert` exits with status 1 and its JSON report has `"ok": false`, with the count in `completeness.unplaced`.
- **Inventor.** Parameter expressions (`Width / 2 + 5 mm`) come into SolidCraft as expressions, and a sheet-metal plate along several edges becomes one edge flange. Thanks to oursland for raising it.
- **The SolidCraft plugin works on a default SolidCraft** without raising Max plugin fuel. A design too big for it opens in the Dutchman app, and a missing folder grant is named before anything is read.
- **Fusion.** Designs made of many components no longer take hours to read (about a minute now). Inserted designs a `.f3d` doesn't contain are named, with a pointer to export `.f3z`. A loft from a face of an earlier model state, a coordinate system, and a component without attributed bodies no longer stop or flatten the history, and a design converted without history says why.
- **Fewer wrong "verified" results.** A replay that loses small holes or differs in shape or topology is refused and keeps the original shape. A history whose extrude picked the wrong region stops before it.
- **No duplicate bodies.** Hidden editable-history copies are suppressed, so a STEP export holds one body per part (RailCore 300ZL: 270 instead of 332).
- **Smaller fixes.** FreeCAD's own errors are reported apart from conversion losses; an empty STEP file says so; `convert-all` creates the folder for `--journal` and `--report-anon`; "Review and send report" sits beside "File a GitHub issue"; unchanged designs are evaluated once.

## Known issues

- Some Fusion assemblies, for example ones whose parts are linked inside the archive, report "N parts couldn't be placed": reading those positions is next ([dutchman-cad/dutchman#858](https://github.com/dutchman-cad/dutchman/issues/858), [cadmpeg/cadmpeg#1880](https://github.com/cadmpeg/cadmpeg/issues/1880)).
- Some partial histories now convert as a flattened shape instead, because their rebuilt body did not match the source (for example a FreeCAD Pocket that cuts the wrong way). The result is the correct shape without editable history.
- The BMG gear assembly reports 8 parts whose positions cannot be read yet (they were silently placed at the origin before).
- If the in-app update stalls, download Dutchman from the site instead: <https://dutchmancad.xyz/download>.
