Dutchman Convert 0.3.1 tells you what an Onshape fetch will cost, shows a partly converted history in the timeline, and keeps more Fusion and Inventor history editable.

## What's new

- **Onshape fetches say what they cost.** Before it spends anything, the fetch estimates its API calls (Onshape's Free plan allows 2,500 a year) and asks first above 50. Stop keeps what was fetched, Continue fetching sends only the missing calls, and the Results card counts calls as they go. Rows and files are named after your design, such as "Bracket - Part Studio 1".
- **A partly converted history is now visible.** The features Dutchman can rebuild come first in the timeline, then a Remove of their bodies, then "Rest of the design (from ...)" holding the original shape. Thanks to f15sim for the design that showed it.
- **More history stays editable.** Offset Face works when its body has two names, Move Body maps to SolidCraft's body move, and holes that end at a flat face with curved edges, two-sided extrudes on one side of the sketch, extrudes whose sketch lives in another design, and lofts from a face of an earlier model state all rebuild. The RailCore 300ZL, 300ZLT and ZL v1 keep 9, 9 and 21 more features; the Keystone Cutter rebuilds 6 of 8 instead of 2. Inventor parameter expressions (`Width / 2 + 5 mm`) come in as expressions (thanks to oursland for raising it), and an Inventor sheet-metal plate along several edges becomes one edge flange.
- **Fusion tool libraries to CamCraft.** A `.tools` or `.json` library converts to a CamCraft `.camtools`, lathe tools included; each tool or field CamCraft has no place for is named in the report.
- **More honest results.** A design whose parts couldn't be placed says "N parts couldn't be placed" instead of calling it verified; `dutchman convert` then exits with status 1 and reports `"ok": false` with the count in `completeness.unplaced` (scripts that treated any written design as success should check this). A replay that loses small holes or differs in shape or topology is refused and keeps the original shape, and Fusion `.f3d` assemblies name the inserted designs they don't contain.
- **The SolidCraft plugin works on a default SolidCraft** without raising Max plugin fuel; a design too big for it opens in the Dutchman app, and a missing folder grant is named before anything is read. Dutchman now targets SolidCraft 0.48 by default; designs still open in 0.18.2 and later.
- **Smaller fixes.** Fusion designs made of many components read in about a minute instead of hours; hidden editable-history copies no longer double the bodies in a STEP export (RailCore 300ZL: 270 instead of 332); FreeCAD's own errors are reported apart from conversion losses; an empty STEP file says so; `convert-all` creates the folders for `--journal` and `--report-anon`, and its counts-only report says which Inventor release saved each file; "Review and send report" sits beside "File a GitHub issue"; unchanged designs are evaluated once. The in-app update no longer hangs behind a firewall that blocks GitHub: it gives up after a short wait, keeps your installed version and links to the manual download.

## Plugin on SolidCraft 0.48

The Dutchman plugin works as before on earlier SolidCraft versions. On SolidCraft 0.48 it also gets:

- A new version of the plugin opens faster the first time.
- Its fuel request is shown and approved when you install it, so it works on a default SolidCraft.
- The operations panel shows how far a conversion has got, through the parts and the history check, and Cancel stops it at the next step.

## Known issues

- Some Fusion assemblies, for example ones whose parts are linked inside the archive, report "N parts couldn't be placed": reading those positions is next ([dutchman-cad/dutchman#858](https://github.com/dutchman-cad/dutchman/issues/858), [cadmpeg/cadmpeg#1880](https://github.com/cadmpeg/cadmpeg/issues/1880)).
- Some partial histories now convert as a flattened shape instead, because their rebuilt body did not match the source (for example a FreeCAD Pocket that cuts the wrong way). The result is the correct shape without editable history.
- The BMG gear assembly reports 8 parts whose positions cannot be read yet (they were silently placed at the origin before).
- If the in-app update stalls, download Dutchman from the site instead: <https://dutchmancad.xyz/download>.
- The Windows installer and programs are unsigned. On Windows 11 with Smart App Control on, Windows blocks them with no **Run anyway** button; turning Smart App Control off in Windows Security lets them run, and on some Windows versions it cannot be turned back on without a reset ([how](https://github.com/dutchman-cad/dutchman/blob/main/docs/install.md#smart-app-control)).
