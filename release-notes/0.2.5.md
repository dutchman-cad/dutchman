Dutchman Convert turns designs from other CAD programs into native SolidCraft designs, keeping as much of their feature history, sketches, and assemblies as it can. 0.2.5 places Fusion assemblies closer to the design, writes FreeCAD drawing pages as DXF, and targets the SolidCraft you have installed.

Made for the current SolidCraft (any release from the last two weeks); older versions get simplified features, or pass --solidcraft-version.

## What's new

- **Fusion assemblies placed closer to the design.** Components own the bodies their body containers hold, and a component placed several times takes the same pose from its operation table at every placement. A large printer frame now converts in about half the time.
- **FreeCAD drawings as DXF.** `dutchman convert part.FCStd --to dxf` writes each TechDraw page as its own DXF file, in millimetre page space, with its template frame, title-block text, dimension labels and values, annotations, and view lines on separate visible, hidden, and center layers. `--page NAME` picks one page, and the JSON report counts what each page kept and lists what it could not carry.
- **Targets the SolidCraft you have.** Dutchman now targets the SolidCraft it finds on your computer, even when history checking is off, or else the newest release it knows (0.26.0). Choose an older one with `--solidcraft-version`.
- **More Inventor read.** Derived parts keep their stored bodies, two-section lofts are read, and a rectangular pattern with suppressed instances reports how many positions it skips instead of building the full grid.

## Known issue

A very large Fusion assembly can need more than 24 GB of memory to convert ([#227](https://github.com/dutchman-cad/dutchman/issues/227)); on a machine with less, the system stops the conversion before it writes anything.
